# Versioned State, Scale and Shape queries

The current complete combustion profile selects weighted State, signed multiscale Scale and finite-primary Shape. Legacy equal-node State, graph cross-spectrum and persistence metrics retain their own identities for replay. A package comparison on one case would not isolate each family's learning effect or establish governing-equation validity. The earlier sparse-versus-native study measured query sensitivity; its all-leaf admission gate is superseded by the current bounded evaluation protocol. No new learned benefit is claimed here.

## Opt-in Round 8 computation and output repair

Round 8 adds generic field-indexed computation without changing the historical family loaders or selecting a new default. `coherence/round8_queries.py` represents all unordered field pairs by integer IDs and shares one self-field and three replacement mutual draws between Scale and Shape. The detached priority mixture retains at least half uniform exploration, coalesces duplicate work with its multiplicity, bounds resident priorities by `max(64, 8*C)` and serializes RNG, pending draws and refresh state. Importance weighting preserves the declared additive population objective before ConFIG, clipping and optimizer dynamics; it does not preserve the learned trajectory.

`coherence/round8_scale.py` samples only additive signed and rho leaves. The complete joint spectral-plus-mean and log-power-vector anchors remain intact. `coherence/round8_shape.py` builds only selected sign/line filtrations and batches their persistence work; pooled positive TRAIN scales define the separate `round8_lazy_pooled_fair_shape_v1` observer. The v2 query protocol samples one uniform sign per selected self field and one uniform sign combination plus a line with its declared quadrature probability per selected pair. A separate checkpointed inner RNG preserves the outer stream; duplicate outer IDs share one inner leaf and keep their multiplicity, preserving expectation with changed variance. Pooled calibration still visits all bounded sign/line categories. These operations retain linear all-field work and make no constant-total-cost claim. The original complete fair Shape remains the common outcome observer.

`coherence/round8_state.py` selects four diverse and four ranked-hardness directions from the original TRAIN/SOURCE bank. It embeds all members and the reference on their merged weighted CDF intervals, so every reference/member distance uses one exact common representation. Warmup and control use the same quantile reuse with all retained directions; only the candidate uses the selected bank. The marginal channels remain. `training/round8_calibration.py` measures eight internal parameter-gradient blocks at epochs 1, 3, 6, 8 and 10, then freezes bounded coefficients. State coefficients act inside the common distance; its channel telemetry is a chain-rule gradient decomposition, not additive losses.

`training/round8_policy.py` defines member-averaged native risk and its Horvitz–Thompson or replacement estimators, and blends verified SOURCE/repair outputs using one global coefficient. The controller uses field-normalized risks with a separately declared denominator floor; its zero absolute allowance never enlarges a zero SOURCE bound. Deployment policy is fitted only from TRAIN and evaluated on a fixed prospective ASSESS panel. A same-panel quadratic bound is not an unseen-condition guarantee. Intermediate blends require both model passes, whose inference latency and resident memory are measured.

`training/round8_runtime.py` installs these components through the existing three-family ConFIG and dense target-risk Lagrangian path. Runtime artifacts bind field order, SOURCE, normalizer, TRAIN IDs, physical measure, directions, units and calibration probes. Checkpoint recovery preserves optimizer/dual/RNG state and archives uncheckpointed history, calibration and exposure tails. Loss curves refresh from recorded histories at the first update, every ten completed epochs and assessment/recovery/completion boundaries.

The `round8_training_runtime_v3_common_stream` protocol freezes the common warmup stream before training: the first update of epochs 1, 3, 6, 8 and 10 uses the declared fixed batch, with ordinary step-based rollout seeds. Calibration reuses those ordinary output graphs with its isolated query sampler and gradient-only measurements; enabling probes adds no model forward or optimizer/dual update and leaves this same declared training trajectory unchanged. Checkpoints and qualification bind the stream identities; obsolete protocols cannot initialize the epoch-10 fork. Warmup/control histories record retained family scores as equal raw and calibrated telemetry.

The campaign tools in `scripts/run_round8.py` and `scripts/launch_round8.py` preserve immutable execution snapshots, verify physical-device ownership, charge occupied GPU intervals additively, and require qualified artifacts and a measured resource forecast before a formal launch. The native common-observer bridge in `evaluation/round8_native_observers.py` explicitly identifies its frozen native spectral operator and TRAIN calibrations; native and Q4096 observer scores have different support-specific identities. No numerical identity across those supports or learned cross-system benefit follows from the generic interface. The numerical qualification script and `tests/test_round8_*.py` cover bounded scaling, estimator/gradient parity, anchors, coupling, fidelity, calibration and recovery. Learning and adoption results belong in the completed campaign report.

In the Round 8 combustion comparison, ASSESS predictions and physical field risks use all 40,300 native sites of each selected condition. Common fair Shape maps those native values by exact physical-cell overlap to the frozen 32x128 raster and retains the Round 7 TRAIN transform and leaf scales. This is distinct from computing persistence on the native 100x403 vertex complex. TRAIN uses the frozen sparse-IDW-to-physical-overlap map on the same raster dimensions. State, Scale and Shape retain their declared individual representations; native input coverage does not make every descriptor a native-resolution descriptor.

The completed matched 1,000-epoch comparison selects the frozen C8 endpoint with global SOURCE-output coefficient 0.5 for this combustion panel. Its ASSESS gains in positive SOURCE score units are State 1.7801%, complete Scale 1.2675% and fair Shape 7.2277%; the largest field member-MSE ratio is 1.007900 (temperature). All three TRAIN-nominated blends passed the frozen scientific rule. C8 had the lowest measured same-device J2 latency, 0.2980 seconds versus SOURCE's 0.1525 seconds (1.9548 times), with 1 GiB peak resident memory including both models. This operational selection uses the fixed ASSESS panel and five existing benchmark requests; it is not an independent post-selection generalization estimate. See the [completed report](../cases/turbulent_combustion/runs/coherence_compute_round8/comparison/final_assembly/report/REPORT.md).

The C8 computation package remains opt-in research tooling. Its amortized occupied GPU cost was 3.49% lower than B8, with quality preserved on TRAIN and ASSESS, but the ASSESS raw-endpoint gain did not meet a materiality threshold and the saving was below the required 10%. Therefore it does not become the computation default. The anchored C8 checkpoint remains a qualified case-specific operational endpoint. Neither C8 nor T2000 meets the additional strong-Scale claim against reference-anchored B8. The campaign used 23.5077 additive occupied GPU-hours, 74 disposable qualification attempts and 25,870 unique formal updates with zero replay. Generic query and estimator qualification establishes tested numerical contracts, without learned cross-system or seed-robustness claims.

## State: marginal and mixed occupation

`configs/coherence/state_mixed_weighted.yaml` selects `global_distribution.definition: marginal_mixed_weighted_v3`. Each endpoint is an occupation law with explicit, nonnegative physical nodal masses. For every field and each of 32 fixed scrambled-Sobol-to-Gaussian mixed unit directions, sort values and their own endpoint masses together; merge the two cumulative-mass breakpoints and integrate the squared quantile gap. Both endpoints use the same frozen TRAIN model normalization; prediction and reference masses are supplied independently. A one-field profile executes its marginal alone. Zero masses and masks are supported; stable sorting defines a deterministic branch at ties, with value gradients away from changes in ordering. TRAIN-bank references are unsupported until they provide their own masses.

For the complete nonperiodic combustion grid, native masses are products of trapezoidal nodal widths derived from raw rectilinear coordinates; the outermost nodes bound the domain. The declared raw coordinate and field units are unknown. A fixed uniform-without-replacement Q4096 panel uses its selected native masses in a self-normalized inclusion estimator. It approximates the full-native physical occupation; normalization of retained masses does not make the sparse panel exact. The native weights, support, provider and fingerprint are frozen in the family artifact.

`configs/coherence/state_mixed_mean.yaml` retains `marginal_mixed_mean_v2`, which asks the same marginal and mixed-bank questions with equal selected-node mass. Uniform v3 masses reproduce v2. These queries describe occupation separately per snapshot and do not localize arrangements. Independent-channel permutations and three-field parity can alter the mixed score without changing marginals or all pair occupations; fieldwise fidelity remains an independent requirement. Named raw-value and own-rank pair occupations are available independently of pair training. The historical component name `cross.joint_topk_swd` remains in saved schemas; `score_definition` distinguishes the mean-bank query. Pooled joint histograms are occupation diagnostics, not the average per-snapshot State loss.

## Scale: signed conditional fields, including means

The new `multiscale_spectrum` family is independent of legacy `cross_spectrum`. Its FEM basis is orthonormal in the lumped physical mass M. For each connected piece it removes the mass-weighted field mean and applies frozen smooth spectral windows to the remaining coefficients. The geometry artifact records coordinates/metric, support, pieces, mode cutoff, windows and TRAIN calibration. Graph fallback has graph scales and uniform initial node mass, not physical wavelengths.

For IID conditional draws, the fair energy score is

```text
ES = mean_j d(X_j, target)
     - sum_{j<k} d(X_j, X_k) / [J*(J-1)] .
```

The candidate uses two differentiable target-free draws, alpha 1, axes and signed pair projections, a positive full joint score weight of 0.25, and no coherence-CRPS auxiliary. State and Shape reuse the primary draw; an independently seeded second reconstruction cannot advance data, sensor or primary latent streams. A deterministic source must not pretend to have two independent draws. The historical almost-fair default and mean-free `signed_spectral_v3` artifact semantics remain available.

`signed_spectral_mean_augmented_v4` adds scaled piece means to the same joint energy-score embedding:

```text
d_aug^2 = d_spec^2
          + mean_weight * sum_piece,field(piece_mass_fraction * delta_mean^2 / mean_scale^2).
```

The initial mean weight is 1. Mean scales are the square root of TRAIN population variance plus a declared positive field-unit tolerance squared. The same task-unit tolerance supplies positive band floors for eligible weak or constant TRAIN fields. Calibration never excludes a field or changes a denominator based on generated power. Physical-offset conversion and spectral arithmetic use the configured precision; the supplied new fragment explicitly uses float64. The fragment's illustrative tolerance must be frozen for the case before comparison; the bounded combustion qualification used 0.01 model units in every field.

The primary projected band distances also use coefficient space: for a mass-orthonormal basis, the mass norm of each projected filtered coefficient difference equals its Euclidean coefficient norm, with the same total-mass normalization. Subtract before projection so coincident represented vectors retain the original exact zero and zero subgradient; no smoothing epsilon is added. Spatial band synthesis runs only for requested diagnostics or figures. Value and every-draw gradient parity are qualified on nonuniform masses, overlapping bands, disconnected pieces, means, weak fields and coincidence. Kernel timing is separate from complete model/update runtime.

Signed coefficient-space and synthesized-field joint distances have matching values and derivatives. The positive joint score covers dependence of means and retained fluctuations within this finite embedding. Removed support and unresolved modes remain outside it. Properness of this Scale score does not make a sum with paired State/Shape penalties proper, and ConFIG need not optimize any scalar score. Report spread, skill, signed co-spectra, absolute and unresolved power, and piece means separately.

New v4 artifacts bind scientific implementation/backend identity as well as geometry, normalization, scales and config. Exact recovery must use the recorded implementation. Legacy v3 score values, state and default behavior remain unchanged.

### Experimental self/mutual Gram extension

The opt-in `gram_extension` augments the fair mean-augmented v4 anchor with separately scored self-power and signed mutual-Gram descriptors. For each band, the coefficient-space Gram uses the frozen v4 analysis-mass convention. Its diagonal is transformed with `log1p(power/power_floor)`; each configured off-diagonal is transformed with `asinh(cospectrum/(band_scale_left*band_scale_right))`. All underlying power floors and band scales are frozen from TRAIN. Positive field-unit tolerances must be converted together with any change of physical field units.

For each descriptor block, the extension fits the unbiased per-coordinate variance over the anchor's same frozen TRAIN reference states. The denominator is `sqrt(max(variance, relative_std_floor^2 * mean_block_variance, std_floor^2))`; it remains strictly positive for a constant field. Every coordinate is additionally divided by the square root of its block dimension. With at least two fields, the result is `v4_anchor + weight * (self_ES + mutual_ES)/2`. With one field, the empty mutual block is omitted and the self score gets the full extension weight. Each score is the fair conditional energy score above, with every draw connected to autograd. The two blocks are scored separately; the legacy detached joint descriptor diagnostic is not the extension.

Configure the extension at the root of an otherwise valid `multiscale_spectrum` v4 family block:

```yaml
gram_extension:
  definition: self_mutual_gram_v1
  weight: 0.1                 # example only; freeze a positive TRAIN-selected value
  std_floor: 0.001
  relative_std_floor: 0.05
```

The extension rejects a non-fair or non-mean-augmented anchor and requires the declared descriptor blocks. Its v2 family artifact records the candidate definition, exact config, TRAIN variances, effective scales, floor counts and source identity. The old v4 implementation and artifacts retain their original strict identity. Reports identify the candidate and v4 anchor separately; compare against a common frozen v4 native yardstick, not the candidate's augmented family total alone. The extension is an experimental mechanism, with no implied learning or cross-dataset benefit. Neither an integrated Gram nor its normalized alignment is a causal interaction or a conditional covariance estimate.

`evaluation.native_gram.fit_native_gram_bundle` reuses a strictly restored v4 native geometry and fits the candidate block scales on the same TRAIN sample IDs; `score_native_gram_payloads` then scores saved native draws without inference. The auxiliary bundle is separate from the unchanged v4 SSS yardstick. Native reports retain the v4 score units and provide physical-unit band power, signed Gram, spread and represented-skill decompositions. They include both raw rho and `G_ij/sqrt((G_ii+floor_i)(G_jj+floor_j))`, using the frozen TRAIN power floors converted to physical units; no validation data fit these denominators.

The combustion-specific `cases/turbulent_combustion/configs/posttrain/fragments/coherence_gram.yaml` is a stage-free overlay with the locally TRAIN-qualified coefficient. Compose it after `coherence_current.yaml` and bind the actual shared SOURCE reference in a generated launch configuration. It is not a standalone profile, and the recommended current profile is unchanged:

```yaml
defaults:
  - /absolute/project/cases/turbulent_combustion/configs/posttrain/coherence_current.yaml
  - /absolute/project/cases/turbulent_combustion/configs/posttrain/fragments/coherence_gram.yaml
optimization:
  multitask:
    fidelity_budget:
      reference_artifact: /absolute/path/to/frozen_shared_source_reference.json
      reference_sha256: <actual_64_lowercase_hexadecimal_SHA256>
```

Use the same bound artifact for the matched unaugmented arm. Schema validation checks the supplied path and digest shape; training verifies the actual bytes and complete SOURCE/data/feedback binding. A missing artifact or mismatch fails before optimization. Keep absolute artifact paths and preserve the saved resolved configurations for historical launches. A coefficient qualified for this combustion study is not a universal default.

## Shape: finite and essential information with a fixed sign/line bank

`topology.strategy: cubical_persistence` with `persistence.definition: finite_primary_v1` selects version 4. Its exact bank and calibration API are documented in the [topology family](../src/phycoflow_reconstruction/coherence/families/topology/README.md). Each pair uses four sign combinations and these three lines:

| Direction | Offset |
|---|---|
| `(1,1)/sqrt(2)` | `(0,0)` |
| `(2,1)/sqrt(5)` | `(0.5,-0.5)` |
| `(1,2)/sqrt(5)` | `(-0.5,0.5)` |

A pair filtration is `max_a((sign_a*f_a-offset_a)/direction_a)`. Line weights are normalized `min(direction)`. CO/T has four unary and twelve joint filtrations, each containing H0/H1. Full direct execution is required; legacy/P3 filtration enumeration cannot score this bank.

Prediction and reference share the reference raster's per-field mean/std and a declared descriptor-unit floor. Raw finite F and essential-birth E leaves remain separate by field/pair, sign, line and homology. Finite bars divide by raster vertex count; essential births do not. The new loss uses `0.9*F/frozen_scale_F + 0.1*E/frozen_scale_E` with component and line weights, calibrated once on paired TRAIN/SOURCE data and positive task floors. Structurally absent classes are omitted; a zero SOURCE discrepancy is retained with a floor. Integer essential counts are frozen and must match on every score and restore, including disconnected domains. Diagram symmetry is not positional fidelity.

The generic trainer calibrates new Shape leaves on 32 temporally spaced TRAIN SOURCE endpoints, preserves RNG/model mode, then serializes the frozen artifact before optimization. Resume checks the exact frozen new-family buffers and SOURCE provenance; missing or changed checkpoint state raises an error. Legacy topology artifacts retain their existing strict source/backend binding. Historical artifacts therefore require their recorded code for exact resume/rescore; base/child model weights still load through the strict model-state interface.

The current case training mapping uses `physical_cell_overlap_v1`: derive native nodal control-cell edges from raw coordinates, bound the domain by its outermost coordinates, and reduce using actual physical overlap with the fixed 32x128 analysis cells. On a sparse query support, IDW first reconstructs native locations and physical overlap follows; the serialized mapping is `sparse_idw_then_physical_cell_overlap_v1`, with extrapolation and support diagnostics. This changes the raster observable and is distinct from the preserved legacy IDW mapping. Native evaluation uses the actual 100x403 vertex complex, not that reduced raster.

A fixed native nonperiodic mask excludes missing vertices and their incident cells. It does not support mixed periodic masks, changing masks or arbitrary 3D complexes. IDW over a rectangle does not preserve holes. Raw finite `/vertex` scores at different raster resolutions are discrete quantities; they are not automatically a common continuum error. Physical quadrature and equal raster-vertex measures remain distinct.

## Matched evaluation and saved figures

`evaluation/canonical_coherence.py` accepts TRAIN-fitted families and saved `[J,B,N,C]` members. The `evaluate-native-coherence` CLI additionally streams the full ordered native support, saves matched member and ensemble-mean field-risk evidence, and can rescore the members with one TRAIN-fitted native metric bundle. Use four recorded latent seeds, the same field order/normalizer, conditioned samples, endpoints, support and bundle for SOURCE and child. State/Shape use paired members; conditional Scale uses genuine conditional draws. Full-native support does not remove finite spectral mode truncation. The [strategy guide](Posttraining_Strategies.md#current-state-scale-and-shape-profiles) shows the CLI contract.

The comparison fingerprint binds actual endpoint path, solver, steps, guidance/final clamp, effective live/EMA weights, query/chunk settings, conditioning, latent seeds, geometry, scales and score definitions. Changed sampler, support or normalization rejects comparison. Family artifacts are checked before and after scoring. Saved NPZ/JSON scores preserve all leaves and model/checkpoint provenance.

Uncertainty grouping must be case-declared. A single correlated trajectory has descriptive paired time-block sensitivity; sample-ID prefixes, pixels and latent members cannot manufacture independent clusters or a 95% interval. Initialization, all draws, scoring, backward/update, persistence/transfers, checkpointing, wall time, CPU time and peak device memory need separate accounting.

Reconstruction NPZs may declare `physical_mask`, optional positive `physical_weights` and `physical_measure`. The writer accepts case `auxiliary.physical_weights`; absence explicitly means equal valid nodes. Renderers compute displayed-support fieldwise relative L2, or label an unavailable denominator. Any non-full 2D mask uses node rendering so isolated valid support is visible. Save PDF masters and PNG previews. Legacy set topology renderers explicitly reject new definitions they cannot faithfully represent; use canonical raw leaves and dedicated saved-data views for those definitions.

## Bounded evaluation and decision

The earlier Q1024 and Q2048 experiments measured sparse-query sensitivity and did not test learning. Their all-leaf 10% gate no longer decides whether the current profile can train. Compare native and sparse leaves on fixed TRAIN anchors, retain the observed discrepancies, and evaluate learning against the declared native validation yardstick. The current selection rule requires the 10% per-field native physical-risk allowance and a reproducible coherence improvement on the selection panel, followed by the disjoint validation audit. Report each family, field, selected and final checkpoint, complete runtime and memory separately. A sparse-work count or a lower training loss alone is not evidence of reconstruction improvement.
