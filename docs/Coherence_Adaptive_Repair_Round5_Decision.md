# Round 5 decision

Publication navigation updated on 23 November 2026: report links open the ordinary-file report library. Campaign, assessment and operations links require local DATA2 storage. Scientific decisions and original campaign evidence are unchanged.

Both frozen continuation recipes are rejected at their substantive 100-reporting-epoch review. Retain SOURCE/C8 and close Round 5. The conditional 500-epoch maximum was not invoked. Round 3 and Round 4 remain closed; SOURCE/C8 artifacts are unchanged and TEST remains locked. The final English report is visually verified across all 12 pages.

Each arm completed 1,200 attempts and 76,800 objective-bearing condition visits. All native condition, observation, query and random-stream identities matched. The direct native-GT-plus-SSS candidate accepted every proposal at full displacement; there were no ascending proposals, half actions or rejected training actions. Successful sampled actions did not establish held-out field retention.

Negative SSS/event changes and positive regional improvement are better. Both paths pass sensor consistency and the 10% member-MSE guard but fail the primary signed-risk allowances for CH4, CO, T and U1. Native excesses are 2.04–5.35 times their allowances; direct excesses are 5.45–7.78 times. Both also fail the +0.02 Scale guard. The direct route has useful State/Shape gains, but sampled Scale and field-risk histories show no recovery. Continuing the unchanged recipe would pursue an unsupported reversal of several required margins. The stop decision preceded all terminal task access, and those tasks were then scored from saved outputs without another model evaluation.

Reject optional CQ context reuse because two post-AdamW parameter tensors fail the declared parity tolerance, despite passing endpoints and gradients. Reject eight-group queries despite a 60.66% reduction in finite query-induced Shape parameter-gradient variance: the fixed-query action median decrease is lower than shared queries, failing the required no-worse gate. Retain the existing context route and shared queries. Initial optimizer startup and fresh-process resume qualify without a correction. Reject the acceleration claim: the matched startup comparison gives a 22.19% complete-epoch reduction against the historical route, below the 25% target. The formal mean above has a different closing/plotting cadence and does not replace that matched timing evidence.

The campaign used 10,250.79 occupied GPU seconds (2.847 hours), including 1,591.34 preparation seconds, 22.79 failed-attempt seconds and 64.23 assessment seconds. The two CPU scoring jobs used 267.88 CPU seconds; the separately recorded endpoint-query preparation used 956.73 CPU seconds. CPU inside GPU leases is included in GPU occupancy and is recorded separately rather than added again. The complete assessment delivery window was 1,735.23 seconds, including 1,596.00 seconds between review and terminal scoring for scheduled monitoring and the stop decision; generation leases plus scoring-job wall times sum to 137.83 seconds. The delivery tail must not be described as CPU compute.

These are matched VALIDATION100/J4 development results: 100 of 1,000 conditions, previously accessed on the same trajectory. The event and right-half mass-weighted CO–U1 covariance are reused development tasks. Paired percentile intervals resample conditions and do not account for temporal dependence or training-seed uncertainty. The result rejects these recipes within the executed budget; it does not establish that every direct SSS method fails.
