\[
g_D=(D(\theta;\mathcal B,\xi)-D(\theta_0;\mathcal B,\xi))/s_D-\delta_D,
\qquad \delta_D=0.
\]

Existing total and five-field endpoint constraints retain their +5% allowance and distinct TRAIN risk scales.
