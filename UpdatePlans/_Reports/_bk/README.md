# Historical reports

Adaptive Repair Rounds 1–4 are published under [the campaign's archive](../Coherence_Adaptive_Repair/README.md), with their figures, source identities and evidence notes kept together.

The old flat reports and their supporting context are grouped locally at `Legacy_Local_Reports/`. Their original bytes are preserved on DATA2 under `_bk/research_collections/Legacy_Reports/`; this uncurated historical collection is not a portable GitHub publication. Its local index records any original broken links. A future republication must verify its own complete bundle before being added to this report library.

Archiving preserves evidence. It does not authorize deletion, rewriting accepted results or training another experiment.
