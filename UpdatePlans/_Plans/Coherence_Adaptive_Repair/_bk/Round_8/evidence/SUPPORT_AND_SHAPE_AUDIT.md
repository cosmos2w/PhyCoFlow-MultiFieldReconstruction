## Decision

Retain the current reusable Scale/Shape geometry for the Round 8 baseline, describe its fixed Q4096 support honestly, and make dynamic physical field-risk sampling the general default. Generalize that field-risk path to ordinary member MSE, deterministic J1 and generative JN without imposing combustion's literal Q4096/J2 restriction. Add one inexpensive, fixed, location-sensitive excursion representation to the existing Shape family as the corrective candidate. A new spatial-support bank, native spectral basis and full spatial persistence assignment are deferred unless a bounded saved-output comparison identifies a material support error that those changes would answer.

Fixed Q4096 is a deliberate implementation and artifact contract, rather than a mathematical requirement of LPC, conditional scoring or SSS. Rebuilding geometric operators every epoch is unnecessary for the recommended change. The current Round 7 implementation already has rotating field feedback and a corrected training-law reference; its earlier reference mismatch must not be reported as still unresolved.

## 3. What is necessary, what can change, and what should wait

The theory fixes an observation map, including geometry, calibration and units, so that its score has a declared target. It does not demand one immutable spatial subset for every possible observation map. A finite library of support-indexed fixed maps with a target-independent sampling law defines a legitimate average of their scores. That average measures the selected family of observations; it is generally **not an unbiased estimator of the old native nonlinear score**. PH, weighted quantiles, normalization and score norms are nonlinear. Inclusion weighting of point values does not remove that distinction. The current manuscript already limits SSS to finite selected observations rather than a complete field-law or continuum approximation theorem.

| Proposed change | Mathematical status | Round 8 disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fresh field-risk points, retaining fixed SSS geometry | Linear physical-risk quadrature has an explicit sampling estimator; no new geometric fit is required | Adopt as the default interface, retaining selected-plus-complement reuse where useful. |
| Fresh State locations | Possible with a declared weighted occupation estimator, but a finite sampled quantile score is a changed estimator/observable | Defer from the minimum corrective change; current State remains tied to the shared endpoint. |
| Two or more precomputed SSS supports | Possible, with a fixed map/artifact per support, fixed selection law and resume cursor | Defer unless saved-output support analysis answers a useful unresolved question. There is no current such bank. |
| New native spectral basis evaluated through sampled quadrature | Possible, but random coefficient estimates inside nonlinear scores do not automatically give an unbiased native score | Defer. It introduces a different Scale experiment and extra fitting cost. |
| A new geometric fit every epoch | Neither required nor attractive for the current question | Do not plan it. |
| Different physical meshes per condition | A conditional observation can use known geometry; the present scorer's shared-coordinate checks do not implement this | Keep geometry-keyed adapters as a future portability task, separate from general N-field support. |

Before any support library is justified, use saved native SOURCE/child endpoints on the already admitted matched approximately-10% condition panel. Compare field errors computed from the full native output versus its Q4096 restriction. For Shape, where the domain admits the physical overlap map, compare native-to-raster and selected-to-raster images on the **same declared output raster**, with reference and prediction processed identically. Compute raster reconstruction discrepancy and the new cheap regional features; no additional PH calls or model inference are required for this first check. Report whether the SOURCE/child ranking or location-error interpretation changes, rather than merely whether two supports give different numbers. Do not treat a different-support diagnostic as the original frozen Shape score or silently refit a native Scale basis.

If this bounded comparison finds a material omission, choose the smallest correction it supports: a better single declared support, additional boundary coverage, or at most a small precomputed library. Otherwise retain the current fixed geometry for Round 8 and close the support-bank question. Native geometry is not automatically more efficient or better conditioned; neither engineering elegance nor a coverage percentage establishes a benefit.

## 4. Field sampling, masks, units and model dependence

For native sites with unnormalized physical masses \(m_i\), total mass \(M=\sum_i m_i\), selected stratum \(S\), and a uniform size-\(c\) sample \(C\) from its complement, the member-MSE estimator is

$$
\widehat M_f=\frac1{MJ}\sum_{j=1}^J\left[\sum_{i\in S}m_i e_{jif}^2+\frac{n-|S|}{c}\sum_{i\in C}m_i e_{jif}^2\right].
$$

It includes selected sites exactly and complement sites with their inclusion correction. J1 simply omits the member average. An entirely fresh uniform subset uses the same formula with inclusion probability \(q/n\) at each sampled site and the native total mass; sampling proportional to mass is another valid design if its actual probabilities are supplied. Self-normalizing by the random selected mass changes the estimator. The current SSS batch measure intentionally records `native_nodal_mass_self_normalized`: [physical_measure.py](../../../../../../src/phycoflow_reconstruction/data/physical_measure.py):77–100. It must not be confused with the native-total-mass correction implemented by [physical_risk.py](../../../../../../src/phycoflow_reconstruction/coherence/physical_risk.py):9–25, 60–106.

`physical_ensemble_risks` already returns member MSE and corrected signed mean risk for J>=2, but rejects J1 because its second output requires independent members. The current complement wrapper discards the member output. The minimal upgrade is one generic member-risk integral using existing weights/inclusion probabilities, with the signed correction retained as a separate J>=2 diagnostic; it does not require fabricating a second deterministic member, changing SSS definitions or adding a new optimizer. Keep SOURCE fitting, training feedback and held-out comparison on the same declared risk semantics and their respective matched observation laws.

The design expectation above holds for fixed native endpoint values, or a suitably consistent query operator with the declared stochastic coupling. A query-dependent network can change its output at one site when other query sites change. Reusing a numerical seed alone does not prove a common random field. The RFF prior samples coefficient weights and evaluates them at supplied coordinates, which supports common-point coupling: [pointcloud/core/priors.py](../../../../../../src/phycoflow_reconstruction/models/flows/pointcloud/core/priors.py):17–36. It does not establish that the complete architecture/rollout is query-invariant. Preserve the original observations and member randomness exactly, reuse Round 7's affected checks, and label any remaining difference as the declared query-support reconstruction law. An actual mismatch is a corrective question, not a reason to cancel unrelated ready work.

The physical-mask contract is substantive. Explicit native weights must be zero on invalid sites; the provider verifies this at `physical_measure.py`:28–48. Scalable fit currently requires compact all-valid support. The physical overlap raster accepts a complete, nonperiodic rectilinear native geometry; a strict subset is first reconstructed by raw-coordinate inverse-distance interpolation and then averaged into physical cells. It is a sparse surrogate, not native antialiasing: [geometry.py](../../../../../../src/phycoflow_reconstruction/coherence/families/topology/geometry.py):65–83, 123–205. `native_masked` is a different fixed native complex. Neither route authorizes connecting across holes, treating invalid cells as ordinary zeros, or using the nonperiodic rectangle map for a periodic or nonrectilinear domain.

Weights mean area, volume or another declared acquisition measure. Coordinates used by a model may be normalized while interpolation distances must use the declared physical axes/metric. Field normalizers and squared-error units must be recorded separately from coordinate scaling. For Shape, rasterization, smoothing, boundary type and physical mask define the observed object before topology is computed; matching only Q and random seeds cannot establish common scientific geometry.

## 5. Shape's position blind spot and the existing implementation

Current Shape preserves selected excursions' persistence, but not their physical location. Its scalar filtrations are unary signed standardized fields or a maximum of two signed, offset and direction-scaled fields: `shape_compute.py`:133–177. Distances use finite-angle cross-diagonal SW1 and sorted essential births: lines 179–191. A coherent structure can move within the domain while preserving those diagrams. Boundaries or interpolation can sometimes reveal a displacement, but that incidental sensitivity is not spatial registration.

The repository already records optional creator locations in `Diagram.finite_locations`, with `cubical_diagrams(..., locations=True)`: [persistence.py](../../../../../../src/phycoflow_reconstruction/coherence/families/topology/persistence.py):87–93, 125–188. These are normalized raster-index fractions, not automatically raw physical coordinates or an H1 hole center. Current `LazyPooledShape._diagram_bank` does not request them. Existing [spatial_persistence.py](../../../../../../src/phycoflow_reconstruction/coherence/families/topology/spatial_persistence.py):21–45, 70–117 supplies a local assignment experiment. Its own contract states that additive location costs provide no spatial gradient while the discrete pairing is fixed, and that multiplicative costs miss an exactly translated copy with identical critical values. Its dense pair cost/assignment is additional work, and arbitrary spatial assignment does not inherit the current SW1 score argument automatically.

[Stucki et al., ICML 2023](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/stucki23a.html) explicitly address spatially meaningful topology matching through induced barcode matchings. [Wen et al., ICCV Workshops 2025](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025W/BIC/html/Wen_Topology-Preserving_Image_Segmentation_with_Spatial-Aware_Persistent_Feature_Matching_ICCVW_2025_paper.html) use spatial-domain information to disambiguate persistent-feature matching. These establish relevant segmentation precedents, rather than efficacy for multivariate physical reconstruction or equivalence to this repository's local experiment.

[Adams et al., JMLR 2017](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v18/16-337.html) provide persistence images as finite diagram representations. Ordinary persistence images encode birth/persistence coordinates, not physical position. A creator-marked map is a possible diagnostic, but its stability and gradient properties do not follow automatically from the original persistence-image result. It is secondary to a soft-excursion anchor here because pairing changes and creator motion remain discrete.

## 6. Concrete minimal location-aware representation

Reuse each already selected scalar filtration \(F_q(X)(r_i)\). With fixed threshold \(\tau_{q,t}\) and positive temperature \(T_q\), form the soft sublevel excursion

$$
B_{q,t}(X;r_i)=\sigma\left(\frac{\tau_{q,t}-F_q(X)(r_i)}{T_q}\right),\qquad
H_{q,k,t}(X)=\frac{\sum_i a_i\chi_k(r_i)B_{q,t}(X;r_i)}{\sum_i a_i\chi_k(r_i)}.
$$

Here \(a_i\) is the declared raster physical area/mass, zero outside the valid mask. The denominator is fixed geometrical mass, not excursion mass; empty excursions therefore remain finite. Each \(H\) is in [0,1]. Start with eight smooth overlapping windows, four along the first declared physical axis and two along the second, and three thresholds. The feature distance is the Euclidean norm of the resulting 24-vector divided by \(\sqrt{24}\), with the obvious fixed dimension adjustment if duplicate thresholds are merged. Use `torch.linalg.vector_norm` so identical features have its ordinary finite zero subgradient; avoid a bare `sqrt(sum(square))` expression whose hand-written backward may divide by zero.

For a nonperiodic rectangle, use untruncated separable Gaussian windows on raw physical coordinates rescaled by the declared axis extents, with centers evenly spaced over each extent. A concrete initial width is 0.22 of the first-axis extent and 0.39 of the second-axis extent, matching the synthetic fixture below. These rescalings are declared geometric choices, not an assertion that unlike coordinate units are interchangeable. For periodic axes, use a periodic smooth window, for example an exponential of a cosine, with the actual period and the same window-center convention. Native-mask domains use valid masses only. For a physical-cell raster, use its actual physical cell centers from `grid_coordinates`; do not reinterpret creator index fractions as physical endpoints. A legacy normalized-coordinate raster needs its own documented quadrature before the feature is called a physical-area quantity.

Window weights are geometry-only, positive and fixed. Evaluate each Gaussian after subtracting its maximum log-weight on valid positive-mass cells, a scaling that cancels exactly in the ratio and avoids underflow. Remove a genuinely empty geometric window before training and record that fixed choice. Do not divide by an arbitrary floor that silently replaces an empty region. The feature remains coarse: distinct arrangements can share all regional values. Smooth overlap removes a hard-bin blind spot, but it does not make the map injective or guarantee a correcting gradient for every deformation.

Fit thresholds once on the existing approximately-10% TRAIN reference panel after the frozen Shape field transform. Pool weighted filtration values by the existing unary sign or mutual sign/line category; use its 20th, 50th and 80th percentiles and freeze them. A concrete temperature is one quarter of that category's TRAIN 90th-to-10th-percentile span, with a predeclared minimum 0.10 in standardized filtration units to avoid an accidental hard threshold. Record repeated levels and categories with nearly empty/full excursions. If this recipe has no useful TRAIN sensitivity, permit one bounded adjustment to threshold spread or temperature based on cached TRAIN fields; do not optimize it on VALIDATION or abandon the whole framework. The toy thresholds below are illustrative fixed constants, rather than a completed fit of this proposed TRAIN recipe.

Do not recompute thresholds from the current target, each predicted member or a minibatch after training begins. A target-dependent paired mask comparison is a different objective from a fixed feature map, and does not receive the generic transformed energy-score interpretation merely by using the same formula. Existing [spatial.py](../../../../../../src/phycoflow_reconstruction/coherence/families/topology/spatial.py):48–75, 109–188 contains useful mask/region ideas, but its per-reference normalization/quantiles and Dice costs should not be copied wholesale into the new probability-score path.

For deterministic J1, use \(d_{\rm loc}(X,Z)\). For independent generative members, retain the usual fair attraction minus separation using the **same fixed feature distance**. Add its score to the existing PH score inside the Shape family with one fixed, TRAIN-calibrated positive scale/weight, and report PH and location components separately. The integrated parent workplan uses the existing positive PH normalization unit (one in normalized PH units) and a pooled TRAIN SOURCE-reference location-distance scale, weighted by the declared query target. This avoids fitting the PH unit from a discrepancy that can be zero precisely when positional error remains. A zero location calibration scale calls for the bounded sensitivity correction just described, or retention of PH alone, rather than an arbitrary huge multiplier or a campaign abort. Keep old PH scores visible as an unchanged comparison quantity.

This uses established transformation and aggregation of proper scores: [Pic et al., 2025](https://ascmo.copernicus.org/articles/11/23/2025/). Strict identification applies to the finite Euclidean regional feature law; it does not identify the full physical field law or the coupling of separately scored PH and regional features. The current PH term keeps its own negative-type and fixed-domain qualifications. This is a practical observation design, with no claimed new theorem.

Implementation should expose the selected filtration tensor already constructed by `_diagram_bank` to both PH and this feature contraction. Compute sigmoid masks in threshold chunks and contract them with precomputed normalized window masses; do not materialize a `[batch, query, threshold, window, height, width]` tensor. No field-pair enumeration, additional PH pairing, fourth coherence family or additional family parameter VJP is required. Its scalar contribution joins the existing Shape cotangent/row; descriptor arithmetic and backward memory still have a measurable cost. Complexity is linear in pixels, selected queries and a small fixed feature size. The existing implicit N-field unary/pair sampler and its weights remain unchanged. General N-field support does not mean full N-parameter persistence.

This construction on the current fixed raster is a raster-area observable of the sparse reconstructed field. It is not an unbiased native excursion integral. The point-sampling version would require its own native masses and inclusion estimator, while a sampled-feature norm still would not automatically be an unbiased norm of the full feature. Keep this distinction explicit in training and reporting.
