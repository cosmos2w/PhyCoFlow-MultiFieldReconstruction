## 2. Scientific premise and why post-training is appropriate

The core premise is well founded and is stronger when expressed through finite-error ambiguity than through a universal claim about bad models. `LPC_THEORY.md:277–307` gives exact residual-Gram and equal-marginal examples: fieldwise errors determine diagonal entries, while a coupled query depends on off-diagonal residual alignment. Smaller error in every field can coexist with larger error in a specified relationship. `:313–335` provides the complementary consistency result: matching paired fields in fixed units and measure drives the fixed occupation/projection discrepancies to zero. Thus avoid framing field accuracy and coherence as intrinsically opposed.

The Jensen example is sound (`LPC_THEORY.md:429–431`; `POSTTRAINING_STRATEGY.md:21–28`): if true states are (S, S²), S = ±1 under uninformative observations, the MSE-optimal conditional mean (0, 1) violates the realization-level relation. It explains why accurate point estimation need not preserve nonlinear realization structure. A signed Jensen inequality requires convex scalar functions and suitable moments; arbitrary nonlinear/vector descriptors have no universal sign. It does not prove that coherence is meaningless before reconstruction is good, that early physics supervision cannot help, or that post-training is necessary/sufficient. Training-from-scratch failures may support this project's practical choice, but the exact matched experiment was not located in this bounded key-report audit. Retain that as user-reported historical experience until its artifact is identified; do not replace it with an invented theorem or deny it.

A defensible post-training argument has three parts: a capable pretrained endpoint provides a useful reference and an interpretable field-risk allowance; expensive relation objectives are applied only during a focused refinement phase; output-based fidelity feedback avoids coupling the method to RF, MSE, diffusion or another model-specific training objective. This supports a modular, general enhancement workflow. It does not establish universal speed superiority over scratch or guarantee a nearby feasible improvement. Such comparative claims require matched evidence and need not become a new expensive Round 6 baseline.

## 3. LPC and finite SSS: sufficient basis, bounded claims

`LPC_THEORY.md:73–165` gives an exact finite-grid partition of mixed cumulants under local exponential moments. Local contains mixed terms at one site; Pair contains mixed second-order terms at two sites; Collective contains remaining higher-order multi-site terms. The proof is a disjoint exhaustive classification with absolute convergence for regrouping. Sector functions are signed generating-function contributions, not losses, independently realizable laws or physical causal terms.

`MANUSCRIPT_GUIDE.md:79–105` and `SSS_EVIDENCE.md:9–22` correctly frame State/Scale/Shape as complementary, finite, overlapping observables. State loses location; signed Scale keeps declared represented coefficients/means but omits truncated content; Shape summarizes excursions/persistence and is not positional fidelity. State/Scale/Shape must not be identified one-to-one with LPC sectors. Distinguish fixed-observation predictive law, across-condition mixture, and within-snapshot occupation measure. Generic N-field kernels and model adapters are interface evidence; learned cross-system improvement remains a separate claim.

The foundation's member-MSE/conditional-diversity incompatibility (`POSTTRAINING_STRATEGY.md:30–41`, `LPC_THEORY.md:433–448`) concerns an independent member paired MSE. Current primary signed mean-risk differs. Do not carry that theorem over unchanged: distinguish the signed unbiased mean-risk statistic, finite ensemble-mean MSE, member MSE and member variance. The auxiliary member guard still matters, but the mean-risk constraint does not have the same variance penalty as independent member MSE.

The current theory is enough for a focused methods paper. Its missing link is a reliable intervention under the actual current scoring/optimizer contract, not another broad abstract framework. A new proof should resolve an actual estimator/optimizer question and not delay bounded learning work.

## 4. Shape history and what comparisons can legitimately say

The old successful topological score used CO/T unary and mutual H0/H1, one training line and three common reassessment lines. It differs from today's all-field finite/fair Shape and sampled banks. Original report lines31 and104 make this explicit. Do not compare raw magnitudes or reduction percentages as though the definition were unchanged.

The existing computation record provides useful witnesses (`SSS_EVIDENCE.md:32–42`): finite State directions can miss combinations; legacy magnitude-squared Scale loses sign; historical finite and essential Shape normalization can create large contribution imbalance; finite signed line banks miss some geometry; persistence pairing is piecewise differentiable and can switch at ties. These are concrete estimator concerns, not evidence that topology learning is universally ill-conditioned. Since the optimizer subtask covers new action diagnosis, this audit recommends only preserving self/mutual and finite/essential leaf telemetry, keeping a fixed-query diagnostic separate from stochastic sampled training, and mapping any oscillation to query changes versus real parameter displacement before changing the observable.

## 5. Quantitative and visual evidence that answers scientific utility

Use a compact three-level result, all from one cached prediction panel per checkpoint:

1. Field retention: every field's signed-risk excess/allowance, physical masked RMSE or relative L2, member/mean/spread quantities, sensor consistency. Show allowance crossings and direction of change, rather than only a total error scalar.
2. Optimized SSS: three family changes with fixed units and raw leaf context, kept separate from unoptimized task metrics. Plot sampled histories separately from fixed common assessment; do not invent a scalar ConFIG loss.
3. Unoptimized scientific queries: preserve the current regional and joint-event tasks as development outcomes, and show their meaning in the physical domain. These tasks are reused, not untouched confirmation. Do not tune the optimizer on their individual outcomes during preparation.

Suggested smallest figure set: (i) field-budget versus SSS/task outcome matrix with complete training cost; (ii) one matched GT/SOURCE/child scientific atlas containing relevant fields/residuals, unary regions, joint missing/excess and member event probability or task spread; (iii) complete stability histories and actual-action/dual-feedback diagnostics. Predeclare representative conditions from SOURCE-only error/support strata, including a difficult supported event, rather than choosing maximal child gains. Every reconstruction map needs its own computed physical-mask error badge. Keep ensemble-mean maps and individual-member or event-probability maps explicitly distinct.

The existing V5_1 premise atlas is a useful design precedent, not a new intervention result: `Premise_Visualization_Study/V5_1/REPORT.md:43–81` shows field/residual/unary/joint panels, GT support, precision/recall/IoU, fixed threshold neighbors and unary-oracle hybrids. Its frame 9544 IoU 0.183 versus 0.077 has only 3.69% GT support and both are low; frame 9111 has 8.83% support. These are qualified examples. Lines143–147 show why empirical quantile transport is not an exact dependence-isolation control when it creates ties. Lines155–171 give overlapping cohort counts, not independent trials. Reuse visual semantics; do not reuse exposed examples as untouched TEST.

For generative outputs, calculate event-area scores memberwise before summarizing and visualize empirical event probability, because a threshold on the ensemble mean answers a different question. For regional CO–U1 covariance, show prediction versus reference, signed errors and its fixed spatial region, but do not call it a flux/conservation law without density, units and the necessary governing contract. Scientific meaning can be an interpretable relational query without claiming a verified PDE law.

Generic core versus case choices should remain clear: core accepts field metadata, geometry, physical weights, masks, signed projections/queries and frozen references; each case declares units, boundary conventions, scientifically meaningful field groups/events/regions. Do not build CO/T-specific rules into shared SSS kernels. No new full-population evaluation is needed; matched 10% VALIDATION coverage is the default and TEST remains locked.

## 6. Compact manuscript story

Problem: useful finite-error reconstructions can misrepresent scientific joint-state queries. Explanation: residual alignment and conditional-mean nonlinear gaps; LPC organizes the mixed information without pretending to be a finite complete loss. Method: finite State/Scale/Shape observables and a common reconstruction endpoint, with SSS objectives and separate per-field risk allowances, no replay of the model-specific native loss. Intervention: a clearly specified ConFIG plus multiplier update, validated at the actual parameter-action level. Evidence: field preservation, common SSS, unoptimized scientific utility and measured complete cost. Scope: one development case now, explicit adapter and geometry contracts, later learned evidence on a different model and physical system before a universal claim.

Round 6 should restore and test this method lineage. It need not reopen teacher/student or rerun native continuation merely to compensate for the prior planning error. The corrected question is whether a diagnosed, bounded repair of the SSS/dual/actual-action interface improves current held-out scientific queries within all field allowances at worthwhile cost.
