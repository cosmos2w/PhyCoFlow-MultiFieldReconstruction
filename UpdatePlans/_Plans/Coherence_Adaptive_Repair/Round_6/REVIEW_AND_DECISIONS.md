## 2. Is the scientific premise sufficient?

**Yes, as a finite-observable, endpoint-constrained enhancement framework.** The outstanding weakness is reliable intervention under the current estimator and optimizer, rather than a missing general motivation. Fieldwise errors specify magnitudes without specifying every cross-field residual alignment. For example, errors of a product or a quadratic joint query include cross terms that fieldwise squared norms do not determine. At finite reconstruction error, similar or smaller field errors can coexist with larger error in a declared scientific relationship. Conversely, consistent paired field reconstruction does recover suitable continuous observables in the zero-error limit; the two goals are not intrinsically opposed.

Jensen's gap is a useful example, with a precise boundary. For $S\in\{-1,1\}$ under uninformative observations, each true state $(S,S^2)$ lies on $z_2=z_1^2$, but its squared-error-optimal conditional mean $(0,1)$ does not. In general $\Psi(\mathbb E[Z\mid Y])\ne\mathbb E[\Psi(Z)\mid Y]$. A signed Jensen inequality requires a convex scalar function; arbitrary nonlinear or vector descriptors have no universal sign. This explains why point estimates can distort nonlinear realization structure. It does not prove that relation supervision is useless before a model becomes accurate, or that training from scratch must enter a bad local optimum. The owner's earlier scratch-training observation remains relevant experience; the exact matched artifact was not located in the bounded key-report audit, so this review does not promote it into a theorem or invent a new from-scratch comparison.

The strongest argument for post-training has three practical parts: a capable SOURCE establishes a meaningful reference and field budget; expensive relation computations are concentrated into a shorter refinement phase; output-based constraints avoid dependence on a model-specific native objective. This is compatible with multi-model collaboration. It does not imply one universal set of optimizer gains, a nearby feasible improvement for every source, or a demonstrated total-cost advantage over scratch without a matched comparison.

The existing LPC theory gives a finite-grid mixed-cumulant partition under its stated exponential-moment assumption. SSS provides finite, overlapping and interpretable views, not a one-to-one implementation of Local/Pair/Collective and not a certificate of governing-equation validity. Distinguish three objects throughout the paper: the conditional predictive law at fixed observations, the distribution across different conditions, and a single snapshot's spatial occupation measure. The foundational theory and computation documents remain local sources at `UpdatePlans/Coherence_Manuscript_Foundation/`; their decisive sections are identified in the historical audit.

A defensible manuscript sequence is: finite-error ambiguity in joint scientific queries; LPC as organizing theory; explicit finite SSS representations and their blind spots; a model-independent constrained endpoint update; then field retention, unoptimized relationship gains and complete training cost. Cross-model and cross-dataset success must come from collaborators' actual evaluations. Generic interfaces or one turbulent-combustion run are not cross-system efficacy evidence.

### What is actually computed, and where efficiency can change

For a fixed represented distance d, the current conditional-score pattern is

$$\widehat{\mathcal S}_d=\frac1J\sum_{j=1}^J d(X_j,Z)-\frac1{J(J-1)}\sum_{j<\ell}d(X_j,X_\ell).$$

The unsquared represented distances, independent members and attraction/separation coefficients matter. Properness holds only under the declared negative-type and integrability assumptions; it is about the represented conditional law, not every scientific property of the original field. Both members retain gradients. Under J2, competing attraction and separation can produce a flat or noisy local score even when the population score is meaningful. Quieting a trace by removing separation would change the learning question.

| Family | Current mathematical object | Sound computation priority and remaining limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| State | Physical-mass weighted projected occupation quantiles, with all-field mixed directions and a joint square root before the conditional score. | Reuse sorting/quantile work exactly and preserve weighted cumulative mass; State still loses spatial position and finite directions leave blind spots. |
| Scale | Piece means and signed spectral coefficients, with the complete positive-weight joint anchor plus normalized log-power and signed cross-field correlation queries. | Reuse means, coefficients and band Gram quantities before deriving query leaves. Do not drop the joint anchor or replace signed fields by magnitude-only power to save time. Retained modes and sampled quadrature remain finite approximations. |
| Shape | Pooled fixed transforms, unary/mutual signed excursion filtrations, cubical H0/H1 birth/death diagrams and finite/essential projected distances; apply the conditional score to these fixed represented distances. | Build each live diagram once per endpoint/query; avoid duplicate pairing/transfers; improve query correlation across conditions when qualified. Preserve physical raster mapping, sorting/diagonal semantics and gradients through live critical values. The descriptor is nonsmooth at pairing changes and does not retain absolute feature location. |

For spectral coefficients $c_a$, a useful common intermediate is $G_{b,ac}=\sum_k g_b(\lambda_k)^2 c_{ka}c_{kc}$; normalized signed correlation is derived from this Gram entry and the frozen power floors. This works for declared N-field metadata, without special CO/T algebra. Full-field anchors carry joint information beyond selected pairs; added triples can alter optimization geometry without establishing new identifiability of an already retained joint representation.

The lazy Shape path transfers detached filtrations for combinatorial pairing, then gathers live birth/death values. Removing one truth diagram computation saves only that component, not one third of the entire training job. Random continuous query offsets also limit exact truth-cache reuse. Profile the complete rollout, four backward paths, pairing and transfers before building a large cache. Existing `coherence/query_runtime.py`, `coherence/shape_compute.py` and the frozen computation foundation give the implementation correspondence; their paths are relative to `src/phycoflow_reconstruction/`.

An unbiased query loss or gradient does not imply an unbiased ConFIG action: generally E[ConFIG(estimated gradients)] differs from ConFIG(expected gradients). Shared queries can correlate errors across an entire minibatch, so increasing batch size alone may not remove that component. This is why the missing grouped-query test concerns minibatch gradient geometry and the executed action, not only endpoint-score variance or a full-panel average. Keep inner scientific weights fixed while studying this estimator effect.

## 3. What exactly must be balanced?

Let $F_k=\sum_b a_{kb}L_{kb}/u_{kb}$ denote a calibrated family, with positive scientific priorities $a_{kb}$ and fixed units $u_{kb}$. Let $g_k=\nabla_\theta F_k$. The installed TorchJD 0.17.1 construction is

$$U_k=g_k/\|g_k\|,\qquad q=U^+\mathbf1,\qquad u=q/\|q\|,\qquad d_C=\left(\sum_k g_k^\top u\right)u.$$

This distinguishes three issues:

| Level | What it controls | Round 6 decision |
|---|---|---|
| Within a family | Relative scientific units and contributions; changing a block weight can rotate $g_k$. | Preserve the fixed common TRAIN prefit, physical normalization, full joint anchors and positive block priorities; audit leaf gradients and conditioning. ConFIG cannot repair a malformed inner objective. |
| Between families | Positive whole-family scaling ideally preserves normalized-row direction $u$, but changes the final length and its balance with field feedback. | Keep the current family scales and SOURCE $C_0$ fixed. Report gradient norms, angles, length and relative score changes; do not claim that outer scaling simply cancels or that equal cosines mean equal scientific progress. |
| Executed parameter action | Field-gradient composition, optimizer moments/preconditioning, weight decay and finite step length determine the actual change. | Measure these separately before deciding on a controller or optimizer repair. Global clipping changes length, not orientation; AdamW and added constraint feedback can change family directional products. |

The [original ConFIG paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.11104), [authors' length-model explanation](https://tum-pbs.github.io/ConFIG/start/troubleshooting/) and pinned implementation support this distinction. ConFIG's geometric property is not equality of normalized loss reductions, independent-query performance or finite held-out improvement. Rank/numerical conditions also matter. The full composed update is generally not the gradient of a single scalar SSS Lagrangian; do not plot a fictitious optimized total.

The current primary constraint is

$$c_f(\theta)=\frac{R_f^{\rm mean}(\theta)-R_{f,0}^{\rm mean}-\epsilon_f}{s_f}\le0,\qquad \epsilon_f=0.10M_{f,0},\quad s_f=\max(M_{f,0},10^{-8}).$$

Here $M_{f,0}$ is positive SOURCE member MSE and the signed independent-member estimate is

$$\widehat R_f^{\rm mean}=\|\bar X-Z\|_{M,f}^{2}-\frac{\sum_j\|X_j-\bar X\|_{M,f}^{2}}{J(J-1)}.$$

For J2 it equals the physical integral of the two members' error product and can be negative. It estimates conditional-mean risk under the declared sampling assumptions. It is distinct from member MSE and finite-ensemble mean MSE. The historical member-MSE diversity argument cannot be transferred unchanged to this signed statistic. Keep member errors, mean errors and spread alongside the primary constraint.

The executed baseline is $d=d_C/C_0+\sum_f\lambda_f\nabla c_f$, followed by AdamW and projected dual ascent using detached pre-action violations. This is an expectation constraint with stochastic feedback, not a guarantee that every batch or condition is feasible. Zero initial multipliers at a feasible SOURCE are legitimate. Their reaction time still needs qualification. Changing $s_f$ while leaving dual coordinates/rates untransformed changes dynamics: the initial physical feedback scales as $\eta_{\rm dual}/s_f^2$. Round 3 changed both scaling and rate, from raw `.01` to SOURCE-scaled `.0001` per backend action. It was not equivalent normalization.

With 12 actions per reporting epoch, the scaled integral rate is `.0012` per unit normalized violation per epoch. Its field/coherence gradient-norm ratio rose from approximately 0.0257% over epochs 11–25 to 4.70% over51–100 and 25.55% over451–500. This is evidence of a weak early response and changing balance, not proof that one gain increase will solve the failure. Field gradients, direction and actual finite effects must be measured before calibration.

## 7. Joint recommendation

The selected direction is one measured repair of the existing Lagrangian–ConFIG execution, not native-loss restoration, teacher learning or local rejection filtering. A short joint audit must identify whether the dominant issue is the constraint statistic, delayed dual response or actual optimizer action. Projected PI feedback is a bounded candidate if response lag is supported; it retains the expected field constraint and one combined field VJP. A per-minibatch positive-part augmented penalty would add sensitivity to the violation distribution and is not adopted by default. [PI multiplier research](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.04558v1) and [PID Lagrangian research](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/stooke20a.html) motivate the diagnostic, but their conclusions are not convergence guarantees for this ConFIG–AdamW composition.

Run one new qualified learning candidate by default, reusing the closed Round 3 comparator where identities match. Allow a fresh integral baseline to 100 only when needed to make a changed protocol interpretable; do not automatically fund two known500-epoch failures. The workplan bundles essential diagnosis, proportionate engineering checks, learning and scientifically interpretable reporting, then makes a bounded adoption decision. A failed new candidate would reject that repair under the tested contract, not erase the earlier constrained-repair successes or settle every possible Lagrangian method.
