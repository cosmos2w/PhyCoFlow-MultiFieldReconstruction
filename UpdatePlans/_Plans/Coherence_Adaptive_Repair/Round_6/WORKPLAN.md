## 2. Fixed mathematical and scientific contract

Preserve the current selected bank, positive inner coefficients, family units, reference transforms, native masks, physical measure, boundary convention, field order and sensor handling. For field f define

$$e_f(\theta)=\frac{R_f(\theta)-R_{0,f}-a_f}{s_f},\qquad a_f=0.10M_{0,f},\qquad s_f=\max(M_{0,f},10^{-8}).$$

Here R is the signed independent-member conditional-mean risk; M0 is positive SOURCE member MSE. The existing reference's TRAIN risk, allowance, positive scale and objective scale remain distinct quantities. For native VALIDATION, compare against matched VALIDATION SOURCE risk and convert the fixed TRAIN allowance using the squared saved field normalization. Do not replace the validation baseline by TRAIN risk, use signed R0 as a denominator, clip negative risks, or enlarge a near-zero allowance through the scale floor.

For FFM, preserve independent J2 training members and native four-step Euler reconstruction. The signed score is squared error of the ensemble mean minus the finite-member variance correction; with J2 it is the physical integral of the two independent member errors' product. Member MSE, finite ensemble-mean error, spread and sensor consistency are additional reported safeguards. The primary constraint is an expectation over the declared training law, not a bound on every minibatch, condition or stochastic action.

The three family gradients are computed at the current parameters. Installed TorchJD 0.17.1 normalizes their rows, constructs its ConFIG direction and assigns its length; preserve that actual operation. With fixed SOURCE objective scale C0, the composed baseline direction is

$$d_t=d_{C,t}/C_0+\sum_f\lambda_{t,f}\nabla e_{t,f}.$$

Clipping and the resolved AdamW action follow. Save the actual displacement separately. The composition is not generally the gradient of a scalar Lagrangian: record each family, field feedback and geometric diagnostic without inventing a total optimized loss. Whole-family scaling, inner-block scaling, field normalization and optimizer preconditioning are different interventions; none is silently adjusted during learning.

Use the same SOURCE EMA materialization and child initialization as Round 3, with fresh child optimizer/controller state; saved Round 3 epoch 100 state is diagnostic only. ## 4. Selected controller candidate: projected PI with one measured gain

Use this candidate when the audit supports lag or oscillatory field response. It preserves signed mean-constraint feedback and the existing one combined constraint VJP. Do not replace it by a per-batch positive-part augmented penalty, which changes sensitivity to the minibatch violation distribution. PI is a local response hypothesis, not a convergence or held-out feasibility theorem.

Let delta = 1/12 reporting epoch under the retained clock. Freeze smoothing time tau = 1 epoch, response target H = 10 epochs, and one scalar gain gamma:

$$\nu=\exp(-\delta/\tau),\qquad k_I=\gamma(0.0012)\ {\rm epoch}^{-1},\qquad k_P=Hk_I,$$
$$z_t=\nu z_{t-1}+(1-\nu)e_t,\qquad \lambda_{t+1}=\Pi_+\{\lambda_t+k_I\delta e_t+k_P(z_t-z_{t-1})\}.$$

The current lambda_t is used in the primal action. Commit z and lambda only after that action succeeds, using its detached pre-action e. Initialize lambda to zero and z to the frozen SOURCE panel mean e, normally -a/s. Project the combined increment once. Do not clip signed errors to positive parts, double-count the proportional increment, or update controller state after a failed optimizer action. Source prefit scales stay fixed. Persist tau, H, gamma, z, lambda, epoch clock and ordered field identities.

Calibrate gamma once on TRAIN, using the mature state and its saved signed-error history:

1. Construct a profile lambda-bar from the saved history through completed epoch 100, using gamma = 1 and the stated SOURCE initialization. The actual Round 3 nested `field_violation` is the last batch of each epoch, not an epoch mean (`training/monitoring.py` keeps dictionaries as latest values). Repeat each saved last-batch value for 12 controller actions at delta = 1/12; explicitly label this a coarse sampled profile, not an exact action-history replay. Do not feed it once per epoch at delta = 1/12 and lose 11/12 of integral exposure. The profile is the controller state after processing that history, ready for the next action; z is advanced before forming each proportional increment, using its saved previous value. Positive homogeneity scales this profile with gamma for that fixed error sequence, not a counterfactual trained trajectory. In the pilot save per-action errors and compare exact recursion against its own last-batch coarse reconstruction through epoch 20; that checks implementation and local approximation, not the hypothetical mature 100-epoch PI trajectory.
2. Compute h = sum_f lambda-bar_f grad e_f and choose gamma-probe as the panel median of norm(dC/C0)/norm(h). Equal norms define a nondegenerate probe range, not an adoption rule. Zero/nonfinite h requires diagnosis; do not invent an arbitrary gain.
3. For every minibatch and every amplitude, reset a separate diagnostic clone to the same original mature model and actual Adam moments/counters; do not advance a shadow sequentially across the panel or invoke ordinary resume with changed scientific settings. Set its current multipliers to gamma times lambda-bar, without adding the historical multipliers. Compare gamma = 0 and gamma = gamma-probe. Gamma = 0 is a zero-current-feedback response reference, not the integral-controller baseline; historical feedback can still be present in the fixed Adam moments. Execute one complete clipped AdamW action, recompute all five risks on exactly the same inputs as that clone's pre-risk, and pool sample-weighted paired responses. For panel means define b = (e0 - epre)/delta and r = (eprobe - e0)/(gamma-probe delta). These measured responses include cross-field effects and optimizer geometry.
4. Set target drift t_f = -max(epre_f, 0)/H and W_ff = 1/(a_f/s_f). Fit gamma-star = clip[0, gamma-probe](dot(Wr, W(t-b))/norm(Wr)^2). This is one closed-form interpolation, not extrapolation or a gain grid. Handle an exactly zero allowance with an explicit fixed physical unit in the reference contract, not a post-hoc epsilon allowance.
5. Verify once at gamma-star. Compare the paired full-field response residual norm(W(b_actual-t))^2 against gamma = 0, report each field and the achieved recovery rate, and require an improvement larger than the repeated numerical replay discrepancy. No requirement that every field or SSS family descends in every action. If interpolation reverses the measured correction, diagnose within the remaining preparation budget; do not add more gain trials.

Run one **20-reporting-epoch** TRAIN-only pilot from fresh SOURCE with the frozen candidate to verify controller response, finite numerics, parameter actions, complete cost and resume. It is not evidence of held-out efficacy. Reuse the fixed 50-epoch LR warmup and peak LR from the actual Round 3 SourceScaledRisk configuration unless the action audit establishes a specific defect requiring a recorded change; no new epoch 10 adaptive weighting. The pilot therefore exercises early warmup, while the mature shadow covers the peak-rate/mature-moment regime. Do not require the pilot to meet final field/task gates. One demonstrated implementation correction and restart may use the remaining preparation allowance; preserve its cost and failed attempt. Freeze the substantive recipe before any new VALIDATION assessment.

This calibration reuses an available historical trajectory to save cost; the generic controller does not require every future dataset to have a failed Round 3. Its transferable elements are units, epoch response times and measured endpoint response. Future models need their own bounded qualification, not combustion-specific gains presented as universal constants.
