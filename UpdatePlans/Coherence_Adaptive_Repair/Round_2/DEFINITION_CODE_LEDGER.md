# Round 2 definition, code and evidence ledger

This ledger connects the authorized mathematical contracts to the implementation. It does not declare adoption or substitute implementation tests for the four mature experiments. Immutable code inventories, resolved configurations and actual job receipts remain the execution identities; [execution state](EXECUTION_STATE.md) records continuation details.

## Training clock and information

Each reporting epoch contains 12 accepted updates, each with 64 objective-bearing TRAIN conditions. The full-pass sampler continues through reporting-epoch boundaries and reshuffles after all 8000 TRAIN conditions. Each mature arm therefore contains 1000 reporting epochs, 12000 accepted updates, 768000 condition visits and 96 data passes. `training/update_budget.py`, `data/topology_subset.py`, `training/post_training.py` and the Round 2 driver jointly own this contract; both optimization batch size and the separate coherence cap are 64.

Training observations follow the inherited random T192–384 protocol. Common fitting and assessment use matched frozen T256 observations. TRAIN800 and VALIDATION100 are condition panels; Q4096 scoring and native 40300-site physical checks are spatial supports within those selected conditions. The condition coverage does not authorize a full-population evaluation. TEST remains locked and EDM paused.

For the allocator's actual duplicate rule, let `k` be the outer-cell count, `m` the number of multinomial outer draws, `p_i` their probabilities, `mu_i = E_inner[g_i]` and `s_i = E_inner[||g_i||^2]`. Duplicate occurrences of outer cell `i` reuse one independent inner draw. The exact conditional parameter-gradient MSE is `{sum_i [1+(1-p_i)/(m*p_i)]*s_i - ((m-1)/m)*sum_i ||mu_i||^2 - (1/m)*||sum_i mu_i||^2}/k^2`. `profile_car_round2_allocator.shared_inner_mse` implements this expression from the full-parameter Gram matrix; its exhaustive enumeration test covers correlated duplicates, and the single-inner case reduces to the ordinary importance-sampling formula. The residual inner-draw variance remains as `m` grows, so flattening outer/inner cells into IID draws would overstate precision. Scale and Shape parameter gradients are concatenated to prevent cross-family cancellation; fixed enumerated leaves cancel from the sampling error but retain their measured computation cost. The audit conditions on the declared batch/member pack and does not measure every source of SGD noise or establish faster learning.

## Reserved task and output selection

The reserved task is regional CO–U_1 covariance over native positive-mass sites with x at or above the frozen physical-mass median. Regional masses sum to one; both fields are centered with those same masses and divided by positive frozen TRAIN-truth regional scales. `evaluation/round2_task.py` verifies the physical-measure and region hashes. The task measures signed regional organization; it is not a reaction rate or an identified transport law.

The task manifest, physical scales and positive absolute fallback are frozen from TRAIN truths. After all four complete TRAIN nomination trajectories and both families' method priorities are frozen, each fixed TRAIN SOURCE bank is scored once to establish its family's reserved-task SOURCE score unit; the truth fallback is used only when that score is at most `1e-12` times the fallback. The cross-family TRAIN-choice barrier and matching source-unit receipt are required before VALIDATION access. No child reserved model score may enter bank, architecture, amplitude or checkpoint selection. At final epoch 1000, FFM uses fair J4 scalar CRPS and Senseiver uses paired absolute task error. The reused Round 1 event has a separate, explicitly recorded development role; its original receipt bytes remain unchanged.

At each prescribed checkpoint 0, 100, 250, 500, 750 and 1000, actual blended TRAIN fields are evaluated at amplitudes 0, 0.125, 0.25, 0.375, 0.5, 0.625, 0.75, 0.875 and 1. Feasible nominations minimize equally weighted SOURCE-normalized State, Scale, Shape and reused-event scores. Measured latency and then smaller amplitude break the declared numerical ties. Both final nominations and the latency-based method priority freeze before new VALIDATION model outputs. Held-out assessment applies the fixed eligibility/fallback rule without a new amplitude or checkpoint search.

`evaluation/round2_assessment.py` separates primary field/sensor eligibility, complete-core/continuity guards, reserved-task utility, the legacy operational gate and actual deployment cost. The assessment runner retains raw and nominated identities. The report must make separate source/component decisions and retain SOURCE/C8 if no nominated output qualifies.

## Recording, qualification and costs

Saved configuration and ordered recording definitions own append order, types, units, meanings and family/component accumulation order. Incoming configuration validates semantic content; dictionary serialization order alone is not a scientific change. `training/run_store.py` reloads the saved configuration, and `training/monitoring.py` checkpoints partial-epoch accumulation without rewriting history. Epoch-horizon or permitted runtime-device changes do not migrate logs.

The actual execution qualification is uninterrupted 12 reporting epochs versus a separate fresh-process pause at 9 and resume to 12 for each path. `sequence_car_round2_qualification.py` sequences children without progress polling; `qualify_car_round2_resume.py` compares model/optimizer/dual/RNG/sampler/loss/recording/coupling state and objective-bearing IDs. Measured timing telemetry is checked as accounting data and is excluded from deterministic scientific equality. Loss curves are saved from first history and every ten completed epochs, plus validation/checkpoint/pause/completion refreshes.

GPU leases cover compilation, preparation, fitting, training and callbacks; independent CPU jobs retain their own enclosing wall timers. Failed attempts remain charged. Cost reconciliation requires explicit hash-bound attribution, terminal leases, real checkpoint clocks and assessment costs. Source/common observer work is shared equally within its pair, candidate-only work belongs to that candidate, and nested CPU time is excluded from additive double counting. The upper observed steady update time forecasts the 130-hour GPU envelope; the lower observed time gives the provisional allocator overhead denominator. Final actual cost decides the overhead claim. Missing evidence produces an incomplete cost scope, never a measured zero.

Code tests and literal-checkpoint numerical microcases establish their stated execution contracts. They do not establish mature efficacy, full-law sufficiency, independent physical transfer, allocation savings, or final deployment adoption. Those decisions require the authorized mature and held-out artifacts.
